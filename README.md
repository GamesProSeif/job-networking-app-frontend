# Job Networking App

The Job Networking App is a dynamic platform designed to connect job seekers, professionals, and employers in a seamless and efficient way. This application facilitates networking, job hunting, and professional development by providing users with the tools to create profiles, showcase skills, search for job opportunities, and connect with industry professionals.

# Key Features:

- User Profiles: Create detailed professional profiles, including work experience, skills, education, and achievements.
- Job Search: Browse job listings tailored to your skills and preferences with advanced filtering and search capabilities.
- Networking: Connect with professionals in your industry, send messages, and build a network that supports your career growth.
- Job Applications: Apply to job postings directly within the app, track application status, and receive notifications.
- Recommendations & Endorsements: Receive recommendations and skill endorsements from colleagues to boost your profile visibility.
- Customizable Feeds: Stay updated with the latest job postings, industry news, and professional updates from your network.
- Messaging System: Communicate directly with other users for networking, mentorship, or job inquiries.
